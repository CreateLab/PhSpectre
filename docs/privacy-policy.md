# PhSpectre — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

PhSpectre ("the app") is a photo editing app. It works entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. The Google Play build does not request network access at all.

## How the app handles your files

- **Photos.** Photos you open are read through the Android system file picker and processed locally. They are never uploaded anywhere. Results you export are saved to your device's Pictures/PhSpectre folder through Android's MediaStore.
- **Looks, settings and restore points.** Imported presets and LUTs, your settings and restore points are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- **Camera over USB (optional).** If you connect a supported camera with a USB (OTG) cable, the app talks to it locally to read or write film recipes. Android asks you for permission first. Nothing from the camera is sent over a network.
- **QR codes.** When you add a QR code to an exported image, it is generated on your device. It contains a link with the shot data encoded in it; the app itself does not contact any server to create it. Whoever scans the code opens that link in their own browser.
- **Diagnostic log.** The app keeps a small error log in its private storage to help troubleshoot problems. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Permissions

The app declares no Android permissions. USB access to a camera uses Android's standard per-device permission prompt.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Photos and camera data are never shared with third parties.
